# Plains Village Starter Seed

![Village overview](/img/village-seed.jpg)

- **Spawn**: inside the village plaza, next to the well — beds and food within 10 seconds.
- **Blacksmith**: house at the north edge holds 3 chests with iron tools, coal and an enchanted iron sword.
- **Farmland**: 4 wheat/beetroot farms ready to harvest — emeralds from day one.
- **Bonus**: a ravine with exposed diamond and iron spawns 300 blocks east (X ≈ 450, Z ≈ -200).

The classic "safe start" seed: village base, blacksmith loot, then mine the ravine for diamonds before building your iron farm nearby.

# Cherry Grove Village Cluster

![Cherry grove](/img/cherry-grove.jpg)

- **Spawn**: at the top of a cherry grove ridge — pink petals everywhere.
- **Villages**: three villages within 600 blocks; two connect via a bridge over the valley.
- **Trial chambers**: entrance chamber at the valley floor (X ≈ 850, Z ≈ 120), spawner room 40 blocks in.
- **Ancient city**: deep dark pocket 800 blocks north, under the second village.

The valley gives you wood, food and villagers from day one; mine down for copper and try the trial chamber mid-game.

On seed **10022** you get the best of both worlds: an **ancient city** generation attempt at **block (8, 56)** and a **village** at **block (88, 40)** — both inside 5 chunks of world spawn.

- **Ancient city**: attempt at chunk (0,3) / block (8,56) — ~3 chunks from spawn.
- **Village**: attempt at chunk (5,2) / block (88,40) — ~5 chunks from spawn.
- **Structure layout verified** with ChunkScope's structure algorithm (identical Java/Bedrock 1.18+).

## Why this is great

- A **village** right at spawn for beds, food and early trading.
- The **ancient city** for deep-dark loot, sculk, Swift Sneak and a warden-free farm site.
- A near-perfect **endgame-pace start**: villagers feed you while you gear up for the deep dark.

## Tips

- The village gives **iron tools and bread** fast; convert a villager into a librarian early.
- Dig the ancient city from the surface to **Y -40** — the deep dark is below.

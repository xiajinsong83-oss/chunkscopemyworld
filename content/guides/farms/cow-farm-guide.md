# Cow Farm Guide

Cows give you **beef** (great food) and **leather** (armor, books, item frames). A simple breeding pen gives you unlimited supply.

## How cows breed

Feed **two cows wheat** and they'll produce a calf. Calves grow up after about 20 minutes (faster with more wheat). Each feeding is on a 5-minute cooldown, so a small pen sustains a steady supply.

## Building a basic cow farm

1. **Fence in** a 10×10 area with wooden fences and a gate.
2. Bring in **2 cows** (lead or push them in).
3. Grow **wheat** nearby for feed.
4. Let them breed; keep the **parents** and cull the extra calves for meat.

## Compact/auto design (optional)

- **Two-layer pen**: breeding cows upstairs, a drop chute sends calves to a kill area below.
- Use a **hopper + chest** at the kill spot to collect drops.
- Keep the breeding pair separated from the kill chamber so they never stop breeding.

## What you get

- **Cooked beef** — one of the best early foods (hunger 8, saturation 12.8).
- **Leather** — for armor, books and item frames (the main reason players build cow farms).
- Cowhide drops even from killed adult cows.

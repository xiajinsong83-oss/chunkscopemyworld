Slimeballs power **sticky pistons** and **slime blocks** — the foundation of every auto-farm. A slime farm turns the rare slime chunks into infinite slimeballs.

## How slimes spawn

- Slimes spawn in **slime chunks** (about **1 in 10** chunks) **below Y 40**, on flat pads at night.
- They also spawn in **swamps** at night (larger, low Y).
- Use the **Slime Chunk Finder** to see your seed's slime chunks before digging.

## The farm design

1. **Dig a room** in a slime chunk down to **Y 30–39** (flat floor).
2. **Light-proof it** (no other mob spawns) — slimes spawn regardless.
3. **Spawn pad**: a flat platform (e.g. 20×20) with a water channel or hopper row at one edge.
4. **Kill & collect**: slimes bounce toward a corner — drop them into a magma/lava kill zone or push them into hoppers.

## Key numbers

- **Slime chunk chance**: ~10% of chunks.
- **Spawn Y**: below **Y 40** (the lower the better, and chunk must be a slime chunk).
- **Despawn**: slimes despawn if the player is >128 blocks away — stand within range.

## Upgrading

- Add **multiple pads** or a **1.18+ "slime-friendly" design** (wide floor, spawn-proofed) to boost rates.
- Redirect the drops to a **hopper chain** into a chest or an **item sorter**.

Use the **Slime Chunk Finder** first — one slime chunk becomes an infinite slimeball mine.

# Raid Farm Guide

A **raid farm** is one of the most profitable builds: it farms **emeralds, totems of undying, enchanted gear and even rare drops** by repeatedly triggering and clearing raids.

## How raids work

- Kill a **pillager captain** (flag-bearing) to get **Bad Omen**.
- Walk into a **village** with Bad Omen → a **raid** starts (waves of raiders).
- Clearing a raid gives emeralds, totems and good drops.

## The farm idea

Build a controlled "village" where raiders spawn in a kill chamber instead of attacking real villagers:

1. **Kill a captain** to get Bad Omen (renewable via a pillager outpost or a captain respawner).
2. Set up a **village** (doors + a villager) so raids trigger there.
3. Funnel raiders into a **drop/kill chamber** where they die on contact (fall damage or lava).
4. **AFK** nearby; hoppers collect the loot automatically.

## Wave breakdown (Java 1.21)

Raids have up to 7 waves depending on difficulty. Each wave scales the number and type of raiders (pillagers, vindicators, ravagers, witches). A well-built farm clears all waves for the big payout.

## What you farm

- **Totems of undying** (from vindicators in later waves)
- **Emeralds** (each wave + bonus for villagers not dying)
- Enchanted books, crossbows, and **saddle/iron drops**

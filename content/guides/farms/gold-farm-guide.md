# Gold Farm Guide

Piglins drop **gold nuggets**, **gold ingots**, **golden swords** and occasionally **golden armor** — enough to make a gold farm one of the best passive resource builds in the game.

## Why gold farms work

Piglins spawn in the Nether (and near portals in the Overworld). They drop gold when killed, and when you **throw gold ingots** at them they trade random items (Ender Pearls, Fire Charges, even Netherite ingots — rarely). Killing them in bulk is the farm.

## Where to build

- **High up in the Nether** (e.g. Y=120+) so no other mobs spawn and steal the mob cap.
- Build a **platform** with a drop chute; piglins walk toward you and fall into a kill chamber.
- **Spawn-proof** the surrounding area to keep spawn rates high.

## Design overview

1. Build a wide platform (e.g. 15×15) high in the sky.
2. Place a 2-high wall with a gap; piglins path toward you and fall.
3. Kill them with a **sweep sword**, or funnel to a hopper for automated collection.
4. Optionally add **gold ingot trading** at the bottom to convert drops into Ender Pearls and Fire Charges.

## What you get

- Gold nuggets → smelt or craft ingots/blocks
- Golden swords → fuel or melt into gold
- Trading: Ender Pearls, Fire Charges, Potions, Soul Speed boots

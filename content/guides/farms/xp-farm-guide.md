Levels power enchanting, repair and trading. A steady XP farm beats grinding mobs by hand — here are the three best setups.

## 1. Mob grinder (classic)

- Build a dark **mob tower** (spawn room + drop shaft) that funnels mobs into a **one-hit kill zone** at the bottom.
- Loot the drops with hoppers; XP orbs fly to you as you hit them.
- **Best** for early-to-mid game: skeletons/creepers/zombies drop both XP and loot.

## 2. Villager trading (best long-term)

- A **villager trading hall** (farmers, librarians, etc.) earns **emeralds → trades → XP** on every trade.
- Every trade grants XP — a full trading hall is a passive XP + money engine.

## 3. Smelting & recycling

- Smelting stacks (iron, gold, sand, kelp) gives XP per item — an auto **super-smelter** banks XP while you work.
- **Enchant/recycle**: enchant books/gear, then grind them back at a grindstone for a refund loop.

## Banking levels fast

- **Grinders** give the fastest burst of levels for a 30-level enchant.
- Use an **XP store** (a drop-capture with a water elevator) to bank orbs and spend when ready.
- 30 levels takes roughly 825 XP — a decent grinder does that in minutes.

Pair a **mob grinder** early and a **trading hall** later for levels that never run out.

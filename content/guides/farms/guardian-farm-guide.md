# Guardian Farm Guide

**Guardians** spawn inside **Ocean Monuments** (the underwater temples) and drop **prismarine shards, prismarine crystals, fish and rarely sponges** — plus XP. A guardian farm is the best prismarine farm in the game.

## Spawn mechanics

Guardians only spawn inside the water-filled space of an **ocean monument** (the 58×58×23 bounding area). They spawn naturally even with no players nearby within 128 blocks. The key: they spawn in **flowing or still water** inside the structure.

## Two build approaches

| Approach | Effort | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Drain the monument** | High (sponges) | Cleanest, easiest to manage |
| **Box the monument in** | Medium | Keep water, funnel guardians with ice/air |

### Drain method (recommended)

1. Clear the monument interior of water using **sponges** (or build a sponge room).
2. Build collection chambers with drop chutes.
3. Guardians fall into a kill area where you one-hit them.

### No-drain method

Build **glass walls** around the monument, create **air pockets** (signs/pistons) and use **ice paths** to guide guardians to a kill spot.

## Optimization tips

- **Spawn-proof** the monument floor except your collection areas.
- Place your AFK spot within **128 blocks** but away from the collection point.
- Add **looting swords** for more drops.

## Rewards

Prismarine blocks (building), **sea lanterns**, prismarine crystals (conduits), sponges, and lots of XP — great for a mid-game base. Pair it with an [XP farm](/guides/farms/xp-farm-guide/) for leveling.

# Beacon Guide

A **beacon** is the end-game utility block that projects a light beam and grants buffs (speed, haste, resistance, jump boost, strength) to players nearby. You build it from a **Nether Star** plus glass.

## Craft the beacon

- **1 Nether Star** (from the Wither — see our [wither guide](/guides/survival/wither-boss-fight-guide/))
- **3 obsidian** + **5 glass**

## Build the pyramid

The beacon needs a solid pyramid base made of **iron, gold, diamond, emerald or netherite blocks**. Bigger pyramid = larger range and more effects.

| Pyramid size | Layers | Range | Effects |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Level 1** | 1 layer (3×3) | 20 blocks | Primary only |
| **Level 2** | 2 layers (5×5→3×3) | 30 | Primary |
| **Level 3** | 3 layers (7×7→…) | 40 | Primary |
| **Level 4** | 4 layers (9×9→…) | 50 | Primary + secondary (Regen) |

## Activate and pick effects

Place the beacon on top and **feed it one block** (iron/gold/etc) to activate. Choose a **primary effect** (Speed, Haste, Resistance, Jump Boost, Strength) and at full size a **secondary effect** (Regeneration).

Woodland mansions are huge, rare structures in **dark forests** packed with loot — and packed with **evokers**, **vindicators** and **vexes**. The hardest part is finding one; clearing it is mostly a matter of knowing the rooms.

## How to find one

- Mansions spawn **in dark forest biomes**, typically far from spawn.
- **Cartographer villagers** trade **Woodland Explorer Maps** that point to a nearby mansion.
- Faster: use the **Structure Finder** on ChunkScope — enter your seed and it locates the nearest mansion attempts.

## Loot worth taking

| Room | Loot |
|---|---|
| Secret rooms | Enchanted books, diamonds, emerald blocks |
| "Kitchen" / storage | Food, iron, gold |
| End chests | Rare gear, ender pearls |
| Redstone / trap rooms | Redstone, pistons |

The **best prize** is the **Totem of Undying** (from evokers) and **Mending books** hidden in secret rooms.

## Clearing it safely

- **Beware the evoker's vexes** — use the **Totem** and keep moving; vexes pierce armor.
- Clear room-by-room: light each room, break spawners first.
- **Don't sleep inside** — mansion mobs ignore it and it resets spawns.
- Bring **ender pearls** to escape, a **shield**, and a **sword with Looting III** (evokers drop more totems).

## Best-of: secret rooms

Mansions have several **hidden rooms** behind walls and false floors. Listen for the sound of water/lava and check corners — diamond blocks and Mending books are often in the "roof" and "fake wall" rooms.

## After the raid

- Turn the mansion into a **base or raid farm** (evoker farm for totems).
- The **Vex** and **Vindicator** drops make it a slow but renewable loot source.

Use the **Structure Finder** to pinpoint the nearest mansion from any seed before you go.

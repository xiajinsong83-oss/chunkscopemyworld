# Trial Chambers: Loot Routes & Spawner Tactics

Trial chambers are underground copper-and-tuff structures with trial spawners, vaults and the breeze boss. This guide covers the loot table and the fastest safe route.

## How to find one

Trial chambers generate under any biome below Y = 40, one per ~34×34 chunk region. Fastest method: `/locate structure trial_chambers` or the [Seed Map](/apps/seed-map/) structure overlay.

## Loot table (trial spawner + vaults)

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| Wind charges, breeze rods | Breeze drops (final room) |
| Diamond gear (rare), enchanted books | Trial vaults (need keys) |
| Copper blocks, tuff blocks | Walls and floors |
| Potions (regeneration, strength) | Corridor chests |
| Trial keys | Trial spawners (kill the wave) |

## Safe route tactics

1. **Clear the spawner first**: each trial spawner waves 3–5 mobs; kill the wave to unlock the trial key, then open nearby vaults.
2. **Breeze fight**: the breeze floats and shoots wind charges — stay mobile, block its charge with a shield, and it damages itself.
3. **Don't trigger the "ominous" variant** until you have full diamond gear — ominous trial spawners drop heavy armor but hit much harder.
4. **Bring a sponge**: chambers have flooded rooms; a sponge clears a 4-block area instantly.
5. **Mark your exit**: chambers have multiple floors — place torches or wool arrows at junctions to avoid loops.

## Common questions

**Why can't I open the vault?** Vaults need **trial keys** from clearing spawners, or ominous keys from ominous spawners. Regular picks won't work.

**Do chambers reset?** Yes — spawners recharge after 30 minutes if the area is unloaded; you can farm the same chamber repeatedly.

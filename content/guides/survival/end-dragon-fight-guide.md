# Ender Dragon Fight Guide

The Ender Dragon is the only way into the outer End — and the game's most iconic fight. Prepare right and it's a quick kill.

## Before you go

- **Gear**: enchanted diamond/netherite armor, a strong bow with **Infinity/Power**, and a sword (Sharpness).
- **Food**: golden carrots or a stack of cooked meat.
- **Slow falling + fire resistance** potions help; bring **ender pearls** and **blocks** (to pillar).
- **Beds**: beds explode when placed in the End — a massive, cheap damage bomb against the dragon.

## The fight

1. **Break the crystals first** — the 10 obsidian pillars' crystals heal the dragon. Shoot or pillar up to each.
2. **Damage windows**: the dragon perches on the portal (hit it with a sword or a bed explosion), and dives — attack after each dive.
3. **Dodge dragon breath**: it deals heavy damage; back away as it sweeps.
4. **Crystal healing**: if any crystal remains, the dragon regains health — clear them before finishing.

## After the kill

- **Dragon egg** drops on the portal (break the block under it to collect).
- **Dragon's breath** — bottle it (glass bottle) for lingering potions.
- The portal opens to the **outer End** → end cities.

## Pro tip

Use **beds** as the finisher: when the dragon perches, place a bed on the portal and detonate it — the explosion does huge damage. Keep food and blocks ready.

Beat the dragon once and the whole outer End (elytra, shulkers) unlocks.

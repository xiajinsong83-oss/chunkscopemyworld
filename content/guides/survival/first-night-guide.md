# First Night Guide

Your first Minecraft night is where most players die. The plan is simple: **gather wood, craft tools, build a shelter, and light it up** before darkness falls.

## The first 60 seconds (day one)

1. **Punch trees** — break a few logs by hand and collect **wood**.
2. **Craft planks** → **crafting table** → **wooden pickaxe**.
3. **Mine stone** with the pickaxe (3-8 blocks).
4. **Craft a stone sword + stone pickaxe + a furnace**.

## Before sunset (~the 10-minute mark)

| Priority | Task |
|---|---|
| 1 | Build a **small shelter** (4×4×3) — walls + roof |
| 2 | Place a **door** and a **torch** inside |
| 3 | Gather a few **food** items (kill animals or break grass for seeds) |
| 4 | Optional: a **bed** (3 wool + 3 planks) so you can sleep past the night |

## Surviving the night

- Stay inside your lit shelter until dawn.
- If you have no bed, just wait it out — mobs can't reach you inside.
- A furnace + coal smelts iron if you found some, but don't mine in the dark.

## Day two and beyond

Expand toward **iron tools**, a better base and your first farm. See our [ore mining guide](/guides/survival/ore-mining-guide/) for the best levels, and the [beacon guide](/guides/survival/beacon-guide/) for your long-term goal.

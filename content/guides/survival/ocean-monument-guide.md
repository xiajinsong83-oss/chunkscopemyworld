# Ocean Monument Guide

**Ocean monuments** are underwater temples guarded by **guardians** and **elder guardians**. Clearing one gives you **sponges** (the only renewable way to dry water) and a whole **prismarine** base for builds.

## Getting there

- Monuments spawn far out in **deep ocean biomes** (preferably away from land).
- Find one efficiently with our [seed map](/apps/seed-map/) — it shows structure positions for your seed.
- Bring a **boat** (fastest way over water), **Night Vision** potion, and a **water bucket / conduit** for breathing.

## The enemies

| Mob | Threat | Deal with it |
|---|---|---|
| **Guardians** | Shoot lasers, thorns damage | Keep moving; ranged or block their line of sight |
| **Elder guardians** (3) | Mining fatigue + big damage | Kill them first — one at the top, two in the wings |

## The loot

- **Sponges** (from the sponge rooms) — wet sponges can be smelted back into dry sponges.
- **Gold blocks** (in the treasure room) — hidden in the monument.
- **Prismarine shards + crystals** — tons for building and conduits.

## After the clear

Turn it into a **guardian farm** for prismarine and XP — see our [guardian farm guide](/guides/farms/guardian-farm-guide/). Or just drain the interior for a cool base.

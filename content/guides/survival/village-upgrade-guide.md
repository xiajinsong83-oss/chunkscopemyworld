A village is a ready-made economy. With a few **job blocks** and **beds** you turn it into a trading hub that pays for everything — from food to enchanted gear.

## Professions & job blocks

| Job block | Profession | Sells |
|---|---|---|
| Composter | Farmer | Food, bread, melon, gold |
| Fletching table | Fletcher | Arrows, bows |
| Smithing table | Toolsmith | Tools, diamond gear |
| Grindstone | Weaponsmith | Weapons |
| Cartography table | Cartographer | Maps, explorer maps |
| Bookshelf | Librarian | Books, enchanted books |
| Brewing stand | Cleric | Ender pearls, redstone, potions |

Place a **job block** and an unemployed villager takes the job (during working hours). Each villager restocks **twice a day** after using its workstation.

## Breeding & population

- Villagers breed when they have **more beds than villagers** and are **willing** (have food: bread/potato/carrot).
- Feed them bread to trigger a baby; babies grow in ~20 minutes.
- A villager must claim a **bed** to be part of the population.

## Iron golems & defense

- Villagers **summon iron golems** when they see a zombie or when they have 3 villagers + beds + workstations within a radius.
- Golems drop **iron ingots** and defend the village — keep 4+ villagers alive for a golem spawner.

## Making money

1. **Farm crops → sell to a farmer** for emeralds.
2. **Trade up to a librarian** for Mending or other enchanted books (re-breed/reroll until Mending appears).
3. **Cure a zombie villager** (weakness + golden apple) for a permanent discount.

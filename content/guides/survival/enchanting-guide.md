Enchanting is the fastest way to multiply your gear's power. The key: **your enchanting-table level (the lapis number) doesn't equal the enchantment's strength** — it sets the *pool* of possible enchantments, and level 30 gives you access to the top rows.

## How the table works

- Place **15 bookshelves** one block away (leave the middle aisle open) to unlock **level 30**.
- Cost is **3 lapis + XP levels**. Three choices appear; reroll by enchanting a cheap item (or a book) to shuffle.
- Each choice has a hidden enchantment-level value that decides which enchants and how strong.

## Best weapon & tool combos

| Item | Top combos |
|---|---|
| Sword | Sharpness V + Looting III + Unbreaking III + Mending (+ Sweeping Edge) |
| Pickaxe | Efficiency V + Fortune III + Unbreaking III + Mending (+ Silk Touch variant) |
| Bow | Power V + Infinity OR Mending + Unbreaking (Infinity & Mending don't stack) |
| Armor | Protection IV + Unbreaking III + Mending; Boots add Feather Falling IV + Depth Strider |
| Trident | Loyalty III / Riptide + Channeling + Unbreaking |

**Note:** Mending and Infinity conflict; Mending and Fortune/Silk Touch are fine together.

## XP sources that scale

- **Zombie/other mob farms** (see the creeper and iron farm guides) — reliable AFK XP.
- **Villager trading** with librarians: buying enchanted books and trading XP is the sustainable mid-game path.
- **Breeding + trading** farmers for emeralds then converting via librarians.

## Librarians: the exact-enchant shortcut

A **librarian** villager offers enchanted books you can choose from by cycling trades until the one you want appears. Every refresh costs a couple of emeralds. Once you lock a good one (e.g. Mending), **don't break and re-place the lectern** unless you want to re-roll — a traded librarian keeps its book.

## Common mistakes

- Missing the **15 bookshelves** — you cap at weak tiers without them.
- **Mending on a bow with Infinity** — they can't coexist.
- Wasting lapis on random 30-cost rolls when you only need one enchant — re-roll cheap instead.

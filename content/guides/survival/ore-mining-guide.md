Since **Caves & Cliffs (1.18)** the world goes down to **Y = -64**, and each ore has its own height band with a density peak. Mining at the right Y level more than doubles your yield for the same time spent.

## Best Y levels at a glance

| Ore | Best Y | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diamond | **-59** | -64 to 16 | Dense at bedrock; air-exposed veins get deleted |
| Redstone | **-59** | -64 to 16 | Same band as diamond — dig both at once |
| Iron | **15** / 232 | -24 to 320 | Two peaks: Y=15 and high mountains |
| Coal | **96** | 0 to 320 | More coal the higher you go; none below 0 |
| Copper | **48** | 0 to 96 | Larger veins in dripstone caves |
| Gold | **-16** | -64 to 30 | Extra veins in badlands |
| Lapis | **0** | -64 to 64 | Widest band |
| Emerald | **90** | 0 to 128 | Mountain biomes only |
| Ancient Debris | **15** | 8 to 22 | Nether-exclusive, ~1 vein/chunk |

## Why -59 for diamond

Diamond veins are large (up to 10 ore) and **most frequent between Y -32 and -64**, peaking near the bedrock layer. Mining at **Y = -59** sits just above the thickest density. Many players dig their main corridor at **Y = -54** instead to stay above the worst lava lakes — a small yield tradeoff, a big safety gain.

## Deepslate rules

Below Y = 0 every ore switches to a **deepslate variant**. Deepslate ores drop raw materials only with **Silk Touch**; otherwise you get cobbled deepslate. Fortune works identically — deepslate ores are just denser and deeper.

## Strip-mine plan

1. Dig a central tunnel 2 blocks high at your target Y.
2. Branch 1-block-wide side tunnels every **3 blocks** (3-wide spacing) — this covers every ore pocket without overlap.
3. Light as you go; bring a **Silk Touch** pick for deepslate ores and an **efficiency** pick to clear stone fast.
4. Use the **Ore Finder** on ChunkScope to check your seed's exact distribution before you commit to a layer.

## Quick tips

- **Cave-mining beats strip-mining** for diamonds: exposed cave walls show far more blocks per effort.
- Fortune III turns a 4-diamond vein into 4–16; always save your best pick for diamond.
- Ancient debris needs a different tool — no fortune works on it, but the **bedrock layer trick** in the Nether lets you expose it fast.

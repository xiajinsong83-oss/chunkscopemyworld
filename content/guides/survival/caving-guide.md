# Caving Guide

Caves hold the richest ore — but they're also where players die to falls, lava and mobs. Explore with a plan, not on instinct.

## Before you go in

- **Full iron or better armor**, a sword and a bow.
- **Plenty of torches** (at least 2 stacks).
- **Food**, a shovel, and blocks (cobblestone) to pillar up.
- Optional: **water bucket** for falls and lava.

## Light as you go

- Place torches on your **left side** so you can find the way out.
- Light **every branch** you don't explore so mobs can't spawn behind you.
- Light level 0 = mobs spawn; keep everything at light 1+.

## What you'll find (and the best Y)

| Ore | Best Y | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Diamond | **-59** | Deepest level, deepslate |
| Iron | **15** and **232** | Peak at 15 |
| Gold | -16 / -59 | Deep caves |
| Coal | 96 | Upper caves |
| Ancient debris | **15** (Nether) | Nether only |

Get exact per-seed positions with our [Ore Finder](/apps/ore-finder/) instead of guessing.

## Safety rules

- **Never dig straight down** (unless you can't fall into lava — check first).
- Carry a **water bucket** to turn lava into safe landing.
- **Mark your path** with torches or signs; it's easy to get lost.
- If you see a **deep dark** biome underground, be quiet — see our [ancient cities guide](/guides/survival/find-ancient-cities/).

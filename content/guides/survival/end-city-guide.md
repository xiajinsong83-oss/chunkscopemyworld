# End City Guide: Elytra & Loot

**End cities** hold the two prizes of the End — the **elytra** and **shulker boxes**. They only spawn on the **outer islands**, past the dragon fight.

## How to reach the outer islands

1. Kill the **Ender Dragon** to open the outer End (see the dragon fight guide).
2. Bridge or fly (slow falling + rockets) across the void to the far islands.
3. **End cities** spawn on the larger islands; fly between towers to spot their purple brick.

## Looting a city

- **Tower chests**: iron, tools, enchanted gear, sometimes a **purpur** or dragon-head trophy.
- The tallest tower often holds a floating **end ship** — bridge to it.
- **End ship**: the **elytra** + a **dragon head** in the bow, plus an **end crystal** and enchanted diamond gear in the chests.

## Shulkers & shulker boxes

- **Shulkers** (the floating shell mobs) drop shells when they drop to low health.
- Craft **shulker box** (2 shells + chest) — a portable 27-slot storage that keeps its items when broken.

## Tips

- Bring **slow falling + rockets** — the void is lethal; don't sprint-jump near the edge.
- Clear shulkers carefully; their **levitation** hits can float you into the void.
- A **mending elytra** from an end ship lasts forever.

Grab the elytra first, then clear chests — it makes every later trip faster.

# Exploration & Treasure Guide

The ocean and far lands hide **buried treasure**, **shipwrecks** and **monuments** — and explorer maps point straight to them. Turn roaming into a loot haul.

## Read an explorer map

- **Cartographer villagers** sell **ocean explorer** and **woodland explorer** maps.
- The map fills in as you move; head in the direction of the glowing icon.
- **Ocean explorer** → a **monument**; **woodland explorer** → a **mansion**.

## Buried treasure

- **Treasure maps** (from shipwrecks) show an X — dig there to find a chest with diamonds, iron, emeralds and a **Heart of the Sea** (craft a conduit).
- Treasure chests often sit under **sand or gravel** on beaches — dig around the X marker.

## Shipwrecks & ocean ruins

- **Shipwrecks**: 3 chests (treasure, supply, map) with iron, gold, tanned leather and explorer maps.
- **Ocean ruins**: smaller loot + stone; find them along coasts.

## Tips

- Bring **water breathing** (pufferfish) for monument dives.
- **Marker your base** with a map or waypoint so exploration doesn't strand you.
- Use a **seed finder** to pre-locate shipwrecks and monuments before you sail.

Grab a treasure map, sail out, and come back with enough diamonds to upgrade everything.

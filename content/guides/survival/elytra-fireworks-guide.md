Once you've raided an **End Ship**, the elytra turns the world into a playground. The trick to sustained flight is **firework rockets** — they work as boost fuel, and their **flight duration** controls how far each boost carries you.

## Firework rockets as fuel

- **Any** firework rocket with an explosion boosts flight; a plain rocket (no stars) is the cheapest fuel.
- The **flight duration** (1–3, set by how much gunpowder you use) decides the boost power:
  - **1** (1 gunpowder): short burst
  - **2** (2 gunpowder): medium
  - **3** (3 gunpowder): long, most powerful
- Flight **3** + any explosion is the standard for long cross-world travel.

## Craft a full stack efficiently

To get a full stack of flight-3 rockets (64):

- **3 gunpowder + 7 paper** each, plus any dye + one optional firework star — in a 3×3 grid.
- 64 rockets need **192 gunpowder + 448 paper** + dye. A creeper farm (see the creeper farm guide) or a **witch farm** keeps you topped up; sugar cane farms feed the paper.

## Boosting technique

1. Fall (or launch from height), then press jump twice to open the elytra.
2. **Hold right-click** to use a rocket — tap it in bursts rather than holding, to keep control.
3. For distance: pitch down slightly to gain speed, then level out; boosting while diving converts height into momentum.
4. Slow down before landing — hitting terrain at full speed kills. Use the flight duration to your advantage: short bursts for precision, long ones for distance.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Not enough momentum"** — climb back up or use a launch pad / water column.
- **Falling too fast** — open the elytra and boost level, not straight down.
- **Rockets not boosting** — make sure they're **firework rockets** (with an explosion), not just paper-and-gunpowder stars.

# Wither Boss Fight Guide

The **Wither** is the second boss of Minecraft and the only source of the **Nether Star**, which you need to build a beacon. Unlike the Ender Dragon it can be fought almost anywhere, but a bad arena makes it brutal.

## How to summon the Wither

1. Collect **3 wither skeleton skulls** (rare drops from wither skeletons in Nether fortresses).
2. Place **soul sand** (or soul soil) in a **T shape**: two blocks stacked, one on each side at the top.
3. Place the 3 skulls on top of the T. The Wither spawns, explodes, and starts.

## Two phases, two very different fights

| Phase | Behaviour | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** (full health) | Hovering, shoots skulls, cannot be hurt by arrows | Melee it with a **Netherite sword**, or use bed explosions (Java) |
| **Phase 2** (half health) | Charging at you, breaks blocks, armoured | Hit it with a bow/trident, or keep it in a confined arena |

## Best arena

The classic trick: build the summon structure **under the Nether ceiling (Y=127) at bedrock level**, so it can't fly high. Or fight in a **deep cave/box** with walls it can't easily escape. On Java, spawning it under the Bedrock roof traps it almost completely.

## Loadout

- **Netherite sword + bow** (or Trident with Loyalty)
- **Golden apples** or a **strength potion** — see our [potion brewing guide](/guides/creative/potion-brewing-guide/)
- **Resistance/regeneration** beacon effects help a lot
- Bring **blocks** to shelter behind between volleys

## The reward

The Wither drops the **Nether Star** plus up to 50 XP. Combine the star with glass to make a **beacon** — read our [beacon guide](/guides/survival/beacon-guide/) for the full pyramid and effects.

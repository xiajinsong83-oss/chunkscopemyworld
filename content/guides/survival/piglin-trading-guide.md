# Piglin Trading Guide

Throw **gold ingots** to piglins and they throw back loot — a fast way to get ender pearls, obsidian, quartz and enchanted gear without mining.

## The basics

- **Give**: toss a gold ingot near a piglin (or right-click with gold in hand).
- **Get**: the piglin picks it up, inspects it, and tosses a random item back.
- **Drop rates** skew toward gold nuggets/quartz, but you can get **ender pearls, obsidian, fire charges, string, leather, and even a fire resistance potion**.

## Where to barter

- Piglins spawn in **nether wastes**, **crimson forests** and **bastions**.
- Best spot: a **piglin bartering farm** — a 1×1 corridor where you toss gold and collect.

## Bartering loot table (highlights)

| Loot | Chance | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Endert pearls | common-ish | End portal + travel |
| Obsidian | common | Nether portals, base |
| Fire resistance potion | rare | Nether safety |
| Enchanted book | rare | Random enchant |
| Gold nuggets / quartz | most common | Currency, builds |
| Iron boots / leather | common | Gear |

## Making it work

1. Bring a stack of **gold ingots** — gold farms make this infinite.
2. **Don't open chests or break gold blocks** in a bastion without gold armor — piglins attack.
3. Use a **piglin-safe room** (not hostile if you don't anger them) to barter safely.

Bartering turns gold into nether essentials — the easiest ender-pearl source before the End.

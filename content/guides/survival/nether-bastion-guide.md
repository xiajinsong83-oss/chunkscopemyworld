# Nether Bastion Guide

**Bastions** are huge Nether fortresses guarded by piglins and brutes. They're packed with **gold, enchanted gear, Netherite scraps (ancient debris) and piglin loot** — high risk, high reward.

## Where bastions spawn

Bastions generate in **most Nether biomes** (excluding some) and are quite common. They come in four types: bridge, hoglin stable, housing units, and treasure room — the **treasure room** has the best loot (Netherite).

## What's inside (loot highlights)

| Room | Loot |
|---|---|
| **Treasure** | Netherite scrap / ingots, enchanted gear, gold blocks |
| Bridge | Gold, gilded blackstone, enchanted tools |
| Housing units | Gold, potions, armor |
| Hoglin stable | Food, gold, leather |

## Clearing safely

- **Golden armor**: wear **gold** so piglins don't attack you on sight.
- Bring **gold ingots** to trade/bribe aggressive piglins.
- Beware **piglin brutes** — they attack regardless of gold; take them out from range.
- **Mine the gilded blackstone** for rare gold drops.

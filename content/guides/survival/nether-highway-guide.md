# Nether Highway Guide

The Nether is **1:8 with the Overworld** — one block in the Nether equals 8 blocks in the Overworld. That makes nether highways the fastest way to travel between distant bases.

## The 1:8 ratio

- **1 block in the Nether = 8 blocks in the Overworld**.
- Travel 100 blocks in the Nether and you've moved 800 blocks on the surface.
- This is why long-distance bases are connected by nether tunnels.

## Building the ice path

The fastest surface is **ice**, with **blue ice** being fastest, then packed ice, then normal ice. Boats (or soul speed boots on soul sand) race along the ice.

1. Build a straight tunnel at a fixed Y (e.g. Y=16–40) in the Nether.
2. Lay **blue ice** in the floor center for boating.
3. Add **soul sand/soul soil side lanes** if you want soul speed walking.
4. Line walls with blocks to keep piglins out and mark directions.

## Connecting portals

- Align your Overworld and Nether portals using our [nether portal guide](/guides/survival/nether-portal-guide/).
- A **nether hub** at the center connects all your bases to one tunnel system.
- Avoid spawning a **bastion** (piglin fortress) in your tunnel — check nearby structure positions with our [bastion finder](/apps/world-finders/bastion-finder/).

## Pro tip

Mark every branch and distance (e.g. every 100 blocks) with signs — navigation in the Nether is easy to get lost without them.

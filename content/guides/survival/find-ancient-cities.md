# Find Ancient Cities Fast

Ancient cities spawn deep underground (Y = -50) in **deep dark** biomes. Searching by hand takes hours — the fast way is structure math plus our [Seed Map](/apps/seed-map/).

## How they generate

- Ancient cities only generate under **deep dark biomes**.
- They appear in roughly **1 in 4 deep dark regions**, in 3.8 million chunk regions on Java.
- Cities spawn centered at chunk multiples of 8, so most have an easy-to-find center coordinate.

## Fastest methods

1. **Seed Map (recommended):** paste your seed → overlay "Ancient Cities" → fly there in spectator mode or via `/tp`.
2. **Locate command:** `/locate structure ancient_city` (Java) — works in survival too.
3. **Exploration:** dig down at the edge of any deep dark biome, follow the sculk veins — cities sit under sculk patches.

## Loot route (survival-safe)

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| Swift Sneak enchantment | Deep dark chests |
| Echo shards | Deep dark chests (needed for recovery compass) |
| Diamond gear + enchanted books | City center chests |
| Sculk catalysts | Kill the Warden, or silk-touch naturally generated ones |

## Warden tips

- Move slowly; crouch (sneak) so sculk sensors don't trigger.
- Wool blocks absorb vibrations — carpet your route.
- If the Warden spawns, don't sprint: drop a wool block behind you and walk away.

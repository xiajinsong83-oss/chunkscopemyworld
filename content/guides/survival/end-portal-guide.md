# End Portal Guide

The **end portal** is the gate to the End, buried in a **stronghold** as a 12-frame ring. Fill it with **eyes of ender** and it activates.

## Finding the stronghold

- Strongholds are rings around spawn (see the **Stronghold Finder**); the nearest is ~1280 blocks out.
- **Ender eyes** (blaze powder + ender pearl) fly toward the nearest stronghold — throw them, follow, and dig when they drop straight down.
- They sometimes shatter — keep 8–12.

## Activating the portal

1. Locate the **portal room** (a staircase room with the frame ring and a silverfish spawner).
2. **Fill all 12 frames** with **eyes of ender** (one per frame; each requires an ender pearl + blaze powder).
3. The center turns void-black → **step in** to teleport to the End.

## Silverfish & the room

- The room has a **silverfish spawner** — mine carefully or block it off.
- The **ender eye** in each frame needs: 1 ender pearl (endermen drops) + 1 blaze powder (blaze rods).

## Prep before you jump

- Bring **armor, food, blocks, ender pearls** and a way to return (the portal returns to the same spot).
- It's a one-way start to the boss fight — be ready (see the dragon fight guide).

Fill all 12 eyes and the portal to the End opens for good.

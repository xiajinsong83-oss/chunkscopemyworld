Cherry groves are the pink heart of Minecraft's mountains — cherry wood's warm color makes it a favorite for decorative builds, and the biome is a reliable village host.

## What's there

- **Cherry trees** drop cherry wood, saplings and pink petals (particles float around them).
- **Cherry wood set**: logs, planks, slabs, stairs, fence, door, trapdoor, sign, button — a full warm-brown palette.
- Pink petals fall like a gentle shower; villagers and passive mobs spawn normally.

## Finding a cherry grove

- Cherry groves generate as **mountain/overworld biomes** near hills — they can be small, so explore mountains at the right temperature.
- They're uncommon but not rare; a **biome seed finder** or flying over mountains finds them fastest.
- Use the **Biome Climate Matcher** on ChunkScope to understand the temperature window.

## Building with cherry wood

- **Pink + white** roofs with cherry planks read as a cozy, warm cottage.
- Pair cherry with **dark oak** or **deepslate** for contrast.
- Cherry **leaves + pink petals** decorate paths and gardens.
- It's the cheapest warm wood for early-game cozy builds — harvest a grove for a whole base set.

A cherry-grove base next to a village gives you a pretty home and a ready trading post — the best of both.

# Armor Trims Guide

**Armor trims** let you customize your armor's appearance with colored patterns using a **smithing table** and **smithing templates** — added in Minecraft 1.20.

## What you need

- A **smithing table**
- A piece of armor (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots)
- A **smithing template** (trim pattern)
- A **material** (defines the trim color)

## How to apply a trim

1. Place the **smithing template**, **armor** and **material** in the smithing table.
2. The result keeps your armor's stats but adds the trim pattern in the material's color.
3. Trims are **cosmetic only** — no stat changes.

## Trim patterns

Templates are found across the world: sentry (pillager outposts), vex/ward/tide (ocean monuments), coast (shipwrecks), dune (desert temples), wild (jungle temples), rib (nether fortresses), eye (strongholds), snout (bastions), spire (end cities), wayfinder/raiser/shaper/host (trail ruins). See our [exploration treasure guide](/guides/survival/exploration-treasure-guide/) for where each is found.

## Trim colors (materials)

Iron, gold, diamond, netherite, emerald, redstone, lapis, quartz, copper, amethyst. Mix and match template + material for endless looks.

Fireworks are both your celebration tool and your **elytra fuel**. Learning to craft them with the right shape and duration saves you rockets and makes your launches spectacular.

## The basics

- **Firework rocket** = **paper + gunpowder** (1 gunpowder, or 1–3 for longer flight).
- **Flight duration** = gunpowder count: 1 → short, 3 → long (lasts ~1–3× longer boost).
- Add **firework star** (below) for color and shape.

## Making a firework star

A **firework star** = gunpowder + dye + optional shape modifier:

| Modifier | Shape |
|---|---|
| (none) | Small ball |
| Fire charge | Large ball |
| Gold nugget | Star shape |
| Feather | Burst |
| Fire charge + gold | Star + burst |
| Creeper head | Creeper face |
| Diamond | Twinkle (trail) |
| Glowstone dust | Glow (fade) |

## Full recipe

```
[Paper] [Gunpowder] [Firework star] → Firework rocket
```

Add more gunpowder (2–3) for longer flight; add multiple stars for multi-color bursts.

## Elytra tip

Carry a stack of **single-gunpowder, no-star** rockets for elytra boosts — they're cheap and give ~1.8s of glide each. Save the fancy stars for displays.

Use the **Elytra Flight Fuel Calculator** to size your rocket supply.

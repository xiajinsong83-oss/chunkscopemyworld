Brewing turns nether wart and a few rare ingredients into the potions that define your playstyle — speed for travel, strength for bosses, fire resistance for the nether.

## The brewing station

- **Brewing stand** + **blaze powder** fuel (1 blaze powder = 20 brews).
- Start with a **water bottle** (glass + water), brew **nether wart** into it → **awkward potion** (base).
- Add the ingredient to an awkward potion → effect potion.
- Extend / strengthen with **redstone dust** (duration) or **glowstone dust** (potency).

## Upgrades

- **Redstone dust** → duration doubles (3:00 → 8:00).
- **Glowstone dust** → potency up (Strength I → II).
- **Gunpowder** → splash potion (throw to affect area).
- **Dragon's breath** → lingering potion (area cloud).

## Nether wart farming tip

Nether wart grows on **soul sand** in any dimension — one wart farms the whole base with a water source nearby.

Brew **night vision + fire resistance** before any nether trip — they're the two most valuable potions for the late game.

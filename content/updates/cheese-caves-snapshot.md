# Snapshot: Cheese Caves

The newest snapshot introduces **cheese caves** — enormous, pillar-dotted caverns that generate below Y = 0 in deepslate regions.

## Snapshot highlights

- **Cheese cave generation**: irregular domed caverns with frequent 2-block cheese-holes and stalagmite-stalactite pairs.
- **Slime spawn bonus**: slime chunks inside cheese caves spawn up to 2× faster; swamp slimes also spawn on cheese-floor blocks.
- **Frog variant**: a new pale frog spawns on the cavern floor and eats slimes for froglight.
- **Performance**: cavern rendering is much cheaper than 1.21 dripstone caves — expect better FPS.

## Test in your world

Grab the snapshot, load a fresh seed, and use the Seed Map's new Cheese Caves overlay (coming in the next release) to locate the biggest caverns.

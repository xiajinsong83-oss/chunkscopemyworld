# 1.21.5 Copper Age

The Copper Age release expands copper into a full building and mechanics update.

## What changed

- **Copper golems** return as an optional village mob — they press buttons and open doors when given copper ingots, useful for redstone clocks.
- **New copper block variants**: chiseled, pillar and door/trapdoor recipes round out the family.
- **Patina acceleration**: copper exposed to water oxidizes ~25% faster; honey slows it.
- **Trial chambers loot table** updated with copper gear and new books.

## Impact on your world

- Copper farms (drowned drops) are unchanged — the ore distribution from 1.21 still applies.
- Villager-based iron farms are **unaffected**; copper golems do not compete with the golem spawn cap.

## ChunkScope updates

Seed Map and Ore Finder already ship with 1.21.5 generator data. Farm calculators use 1.21.5 rates.

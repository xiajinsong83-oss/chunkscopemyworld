# Wilderness Bound: 1.22 Plans

Mojang's 1.22 roadmap ("Wilderness Bound") expands world generation again.

## Announced changes

- **World height extension**: build limit raises to Y = 640, deepslate floor lowers to Y = -80 (new deep bedrock).
- **New biomes**: desert oases with date palms, and misty highland meadows on mountain peaks.
- **Structure changes**: village weights rebalance toward savanna and taiga; new "trading post" structure between villages.
- **Performance**: chunk generation moves to a new parallel pipeline — 30% faster chunk loading.

## What it means for you

- Seeds from 1.21 remain valid; only newly generated chunks change.
- Slime chunk positions **do not change** (seed-derived) — your slime farms are safe.
- Ore Finder and Seed Map will receive 1.22 generator profiles at release.

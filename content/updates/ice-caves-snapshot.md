# Snapshot: Ice Caves

The second wave snapshot adds **ice caves** — frozen caverns under snowy peaks and frozen oceans.

## Snapshot highlights

- **Ice cave generation**: layered packed ice and blue ice veins in the deepslate layer under frozen biomes.
- **Frostburn**: standing on packed-ice ore veins deals 1 damage every 8 seconds unless you wear leather boots.
- **Ice spikes biome update**: spikes are taller (up to 40 blocks) and generate ice-cave entrances.
- **New loot**: ice caves contain bundled frost potions and frost arrow schematics in trial chests.

## ChunkScope updates

Ore Finder will add a Frost Vein overlay, and the Seed Map gets ice-cave region markers once the snapshot settles into a release.

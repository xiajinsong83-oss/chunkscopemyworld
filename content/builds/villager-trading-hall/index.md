A dedicated **trading hall** keeps your librarian, armorer and cartographer trades locked and organized. This build holds **8–10 villagers** in individual 1-wide stalls.

## Materials

- 3 stacks **oak/fir planks** (walls, stalls)
- 10 **job blocks** (lectern, smithing tables, etc.)
- 10 **beds** (one per villager, out of sight to avoid nitwits)
- 1 stack **glass** (viewing)

## Layout

- A central corridor with **2×2 stalls** on each side; each stall has a **bed + job block + door**.
- Breeder feeds new villagers into a drop shaft at one end; a **water stream** pushes them into empty stalls.
- Lock trades by trading once; break/replace the lectern to re-roll a librarian's book.

## Tips

- **Light every stall** and keep golems out of the hall (they spawn from beds+gathering).
- **Nitwits** (no job block) get recycled — push them into a lava/fill chamber away from the hall.
- Size the crop fields with the **Crop Yield Calculator** so farmers never run dry.

## Why this build

Locked trades = reliable **Mending, diamond gear and explorer maps** on demand. It's the single best quality-of-life build in survival.

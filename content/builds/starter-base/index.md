A base that works from night one and grows with you. The footprint is a **7×7** interior with a 1-block-thick wall — small enough to light completely, big enough for storage and a bed.

## Materials (day one)

- 2 stacks of **oak planks** (walls + floor)
- 1 stack **cobblestone** (for a blast-safe chest room later)
- 4 **doors**, 8 **torches**
- Optional: a **water** block + farm plot

## Layout

| Zone | Where | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Bed | back corner | Respawn point |
| Crafting | by the door | Table + furnace |
| Storage wall | one wall | 6–12 chests in 2 rows |
| Farm ring | outside | 3×3 watered patch |
| Smelting corner | opposite storage | Furnace + smokers |

## Build steps

1. Lay a 7×7 oak plank floor, raise 2-block walls with a door on one side.
2. Light everything with 4–8 torches; leave a skylight-free roof for mob-proofing.
3. Ring the outside with a watered 3×3 farm (bonemeal-ready).
4. Upgrade the floor to **deepslate** and wall the chests in cobble later.

## Why this works

Small + lit = **zero mob spawns inside**. It's cheap (no iron), expandable (grow a wing per project), and the farm ring feeds you while you build the real base.

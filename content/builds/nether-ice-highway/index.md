![Nether highway](/img/nether-portal.jpg)

Because the Nether is **8× smaller**, a highway there moves you 8× faster across the map. Boats on **packed/blue ice** are the fastest survival transport in the game.

## Materials (per 100 blocks)

- **Blue ice**: ~25 blocks (fastest, hard to get) OR **packed ice**: ~25 (slower but easy)
- 100 **blocks** to line the tunnel (glass for light, stone for solidity)
- 1 boat + signs to stop

## The hub design

1. Build a **central room** with portal frames radiating to each base.
2. Tunnel from each portal to a **soul-sand/ice** starter, then a straight boat lane.
3. Line the lane with glass and torches; add **signs at each base junction** (distance + name).

## Portal linking rules

- Each overworld base portal must map to its own nether portal (within 128 blocks to share).
- Use the **Nether Portal Calculator** to convert coords: overworld ÷8 = nether.
- Keep the tunnel **2×2** and flat for max boat speed.

## Why this build

It turns a 10,000-block overworld trek into a <10-second boat ride. Add a hub room per biome and your whole world becomes one connected network.

The Ore Finder shows exactly where each ore generates, including the new 1.21+ deepslate distribution. Enter your seed and edition to get ore heatmaps for any depth, then plan your strip mines or branch mines around guaranteed veins.

## Best Y levels (Java 1.21)

| Ore | Best Y level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Diamond | Y = -59 | Most common between -64 and -32 |
| Ancient Debris | Y = -13 to -14 | 1 vein per chunk on average |
| Iron | Y = 16 | Also common above Y = 32 |
| Gold | Y = -16 | More common in badlands |
| Copper | Y = 48 | Large veins in dripstone caves |
| Redstone | Y = -59 | Very common below Y = 0 |
| Deepslate ores | Y = -64 to 0 | Drop raw materials with silk touch |

## How to use

1. Enter your seed (optional — the tool also works per-region without a seed).
2. Pick the ore and a Y level range.
3. The heatmap marks high-probability chunks — the green grid is your best dig line.

## Common questions

**Does Fortune work on deepslate ores?** Yes — fortune multipliers apply identically; deepslate variants are simply denser and deeper.

**Why do I find more diamonds at -59?** Since 1.18 diamond veins are large (up to 10 ore) and most frequent between Y -32 and -64; -59 captures the deepest, densest layer above bedrock.

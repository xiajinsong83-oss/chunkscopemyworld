The ancient city is the crown of the **deep dark** biome, a sprawling ruin under the surface that holds the game's richest loot — if you can avoid the **warden**. It generates on a **24-chunk spacing** grid, salt `20083232`, in the deep dark below Y ~ -40.

## Ancient city loot

| Chest | Typical contents |
|---|---|
| Reinforced deepslate chest | Diamond gear, enchanted books, **echo shards** |
| Tactical chest | Diamonds, netherite scraps, Swift Sneak, gilded blackstone |
| Corner / wall chests | Iron, gold, emerald, bone blocks, candles |
| Lava + sculk everywhere | Sculk catalysts, sensors, shriekers |

## The warden

- Spawns from **sculk shriekers** when you set them off repeatedly.
- ~500 HP, one-shots through most armor, immune to most damage — **don't fight it, sneak**.
- **Swift Sneak** boots (found here) let you move while crouching.

## Generation facts

- Spacing **24** chunks, separation **8**, salt **20083232**.
- Must be in deep dark biome; centers on a **sculk** structure with corridors and loot rooms.

Ruined nether portals are broken obsidian portal frames found on the surface and underground — the best **gold and obsidian** source early on. They generate on a **40-chunk spacing** grid, salt `34222645`.

## Ruined portal loot

| Chest | Typical contents |
|---|---|
| Portal chest | **Gold**, obsidian, flint & steel, occasionally enchanted golden apple |
| Frame blocks | Obsidian (already mined for you) |

## Why they matter

- A single ruined portal gives you enough **gold** for piglin trades and enough **obsidian** to complete a working nether portal.
- They're common in most overworld biomes.

## Generation facts

- Spacing **40** chunks, separation **15**, salt **34222645**.
- In the overworld and in the nether; size and completeness vary.

Shipwrecks are sunken or beached vessels in oceans and beaches with **buried treasure**. They generate on a **24-chunk spacing** grid, salt `165745295`.

## Shipwreck loot

| Chest | Typical contents |
|---|---|
| Cargo (supply) chest | Iron, coal, wheat, sticks |
| Treasure chest | Gold, diamonds, **heart of the sea** |
| Map chest | **Buried Treasure Map** → leads to a treasure mound with diamonds + heart of the sea |

## Why heart of the sea

- Crafted with 8 nautilus shells into a **conduit** — underwater breathing/vision within its range.
- The fastest early-game path to a conduit and serious early diamonds.

## Generation facts

- Spacing **24** chunks, separation **4**, salt **165745295**.
- In oceans, rivers, beaches; a third are "float" (buried) variants.

**Find yours:** enter your seed in the [Structure Finder](/apps/structure-finder/) and grab a heart of the sea fast.

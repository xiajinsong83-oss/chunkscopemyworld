The pillager outpost is a wooden watchtower manned by pillagers, topped with a **raid captain** (banner carrier). It generates on a **32-chunk spacing** grid, salt `165745296`.

## Outpost loot

| Chest | Typical contents |
|---|---|
| Top chest | Iron, crossbow, arrows, occasionally diamond |
| Captains | Kill for a **bad omen** → start a raid |
| Looms | Patterns for banners |

## Raid → totem path

- Bad Omen starts a raid on a village; raid captains in waves drop **Totems of Undying**.
- Outposts are also a fast **crossbow** and **emerald** source early.

## Generation facts

- Spacing **32** chunks, separation **8**, salt **165745296**.
- In most temperate biomes; often near villages.

**Find yours:** enter your seed in the [Structure Finder](/apps/structure-finder/) and mark the outpost.

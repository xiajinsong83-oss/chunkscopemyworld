Villages are the most common major structure and your fastest path to food, beds and trading. They generate on a **32-chunk spacing** grid with an 8-chunk minimum separation (salt `10387312`), in plains, desert, savanna, taiga, snowy and jungle variants.

## Village loot

| Chest | Typical contents |
|---|---|
| House / workroom | Bread, wheat, iron ingots, lapis |
| Cartographer | Maps, paper, emerald |
| Blacksmith | Iron, tools, occasionally diamonds, obsidian |
| Temple / big house | Emeralds, enchanted books |

## Professions you want

- **Librarian** (lectern) — enchanted books; re-roll for Mending.
- **Cartographer** — woodland/explorer maps to find mansions and strongholds.
- **Armorer/Toolsmith** — diamond gear trades.
- **Farmer** — the emerald economy engine.

## Generation facts

- Spacing **32** chunks, separation **8**, salt **10387312**.
- Variant depends on biome; a village needs a valid biome and flat enough terrain.
- Bedrock and Java 1.18+ generate identical village layouts for the same seed.

**Find yours:** enter your seed in the [Structure Finder](/apps/structure-finder/) and locate the nearest village instantly.

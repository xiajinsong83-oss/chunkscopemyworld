The ocean monument is a guardian-guarded structure on the ocean floor — the only renewable source of **sponge** and a mountain of **prismarine**. It generates on a **32-chunk spacing** grid, salt `10387313`, in deep ocean biomes.

## Monument loot

| Chest | Typical contents |
|---|---|
| Gold room | **8 gold blocks** |
| Sponge room | Wet sponge (dries in the nether) |
| Prismarine/sea lanterns | Bulk building blocks |
| Guardian farm | Sponge, prismarine shards, fish |

## Guardian farm

- Guardians spawn in the monument's water volume; a **guardian farm** here is the standard sponge + prismarine source.
- Bring **water-breathing**, a door for air, and **milk** to cure mining fatigue.

## Generation facts

- Spacing **32** chunks, separation **5**, salt **10387313**.
- Only in **deep ocean** biomes; Bedrock 1.18+ matches Java layout.

**Find yours:** enter your seed in the [Structure Finder](/apps/structure-finder/) and locate the nearest monument.

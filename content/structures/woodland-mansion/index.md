The woodland mansion is the largest structure in the game, a multi-room manor in **dark forests** guarded by evokers and vindicators. It generates on a **80-chunk spacing** grid, salt `10387319`.

## Mansion loot

| Room | Typical contents |
|---|---|
| Secret rooms | Enchanted books, **diamonds, emerald blocks** |
| Storage / kitchen | Iron, gold, food |
| Roof & fake-wall rooms | Diamonds, Mending books |
| End chests | Rare gear, ender pearls |

## The evoker & totem

- **Evokers** drop the **Totem of Undying** — save you from death once.
- Their **vexes** pierce armor and ignore cover; bring a shield and keep moving.

## Generation facts

- Spacing **80** chunks, separation **20**, salt **10387319** (much rarer than villages).
- Only in **dark forest**; usually far from spawn — the **cartographer's Woodland Explorer Map** or the Structure Finder are the reliable ways to reach one.

**Find yours:** enter your seed in the [Structure Finder](/apps/structure-finder/) to pinpoint the nearest mansion.

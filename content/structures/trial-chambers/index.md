Trial chambers (added in 1.21) are procedural dungeon complexes under the overworld packed with **trial spawners**, **breeze**, and the best repeatable loot in the game. They generate on a **34-chunk spacing** grid, salt `84285879`.

## Trial chambers loot

| Chest | Typical contents |
|---|---|
| Trial spawner drops | Iron, arrows, occasionally **trial keys** |
| Supply chests | Iron, gold, arrows, potions |
| **Ominous** chambers | Better loot, **trial keys → ominous** variants, more spawners |
| Vaults | Locked loot — open with **trial keys** |

## Breeze & gear

- **Breeze** drops wind charges and spawns in specific rooms — farmable for **wind** items.
- **Bolt & Wind** enchanted books are chamber-exclusive.

## Generation facts

- Spacing **34** chunks, separation **8**, salt **84285879**.
- Found underground (typically Y 0 to -40); layout is procedural and varies.

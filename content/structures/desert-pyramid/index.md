The desert pyramid (temple) is a sandstone structure in **deserts** with a hidden underground treasure room. It generates on a **32-chunk spacing** grid, salt `14357617`.

## Desert pyramid loot

| Chest | Typical contents |
|---|---|
| 4 hidden chests | Diamond, gold, iron, emerald |
| Trap chest | **TNT** (the floor is a TNT trap) |
| Center loot | Enchanted books, occasionally armor |

## The trap

- The treasure room floor has **9 TNT** under sandstone; walk on the wrong block and it detonates.
- Dig carefully or disarm the trap before looting.

## Generation facts

- Spacing **32** chunks, separation **8**, salt **14357617**.
- Requires **desert** biome; Bedrock 1.18+ matches Java.

**Find yours:** enter your seed in the [Structure Finder](/apps/structure-finder/) and mark the pyramid before you dig down.

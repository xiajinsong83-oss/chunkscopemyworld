The igloo is a small snowy structure — but about half of them hide a **basement** with a brewing stand, a zombie villager and a cure. It generates on a **32-chunk spacing** grid, salt `14357618`.

## Igloo loot

| Chest | Typical contents |
|---|---|
| Basement chest | Golden apple, diamonds, iron |
| Brewing stand | Awkward potions, blaze powder |
| The cure | A zombie villager + weakness potion + golden apple — cure it for a discount librarian |

## Generation facts

- Spacing **32** chunks, separation **8**, salt **14357618**.
- In **snowy** biomes (snowy plains, taiga, beach); the basement is present in ~half of igloos.

**Find yours:** enter your seed in the [Structure Finder](/apps/structure-finder/) and find an igloo with a basement.

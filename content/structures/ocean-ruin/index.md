Ocean ruins are small stone or sandstone remains in oceans and beaches, in **warm** (sandstone) and **cold** (stone) variants. They generate on a **20-chunk spacing** grid, salt `14357621`.

## Ocean ruin loot

| Chest | Typical contents |
|---|---|
| Warm ruin chests | Gold, iron, emerald, occasionally **heart of the sea** |
| Cold ruin chests | Iron, coal, waterlogged loot |

## Why they matter

- The most common source of early **gold** and a second path to a **conduit**.
- Small and fast to loot — great for a sea-route start.

## Generation facts

- Spacing **20** chunks, separation **8**, salt **14357621**.
- Warm/cold variant set by biome temperature.

**Find yours:** enter your seed in the [Structure Finder](/apps/structure-finder/) and sweep the nearest ruins.

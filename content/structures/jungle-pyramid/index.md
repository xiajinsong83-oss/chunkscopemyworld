The jungle pyramid (temple) is a mossy structure in **jungles** with a secret underground room and arrow-dispenser traps. It generates on a **32-chunk spacing** grid, salt `14357619`.

## Jungle pyramid loot

| Chest | Typical contents |
|---|---|
| 2 hidden chests | Diamond, gold, iron, bones |
| Trap | Dispensers with arrows trigger as you descend |

## Generation facts

- Spacing **32** chunks, separation **8**, salt **14357619**.
- Requires **jungle** biome; rarer than desert temples.

**Find yours:** enter your seed in the [Structure Finder](/apps/structure-finder/) and mark the pyramid before descending.

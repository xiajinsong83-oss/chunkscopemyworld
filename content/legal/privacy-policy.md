# Privacy Policy

*Effective: 2026-01-01*

## Short version

ChunkScope runs entirely in your browser. We collect **no personal data** unless you contact us or subscribe to the newsletter. We use third-party advertising (Google AdSense) which may set cookies.

## What we collect

| Data | Purpose | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Newsletter email | Send version updates & new tools | Your consent |
| Contact emails | Answer your questions | Your request |
| Anonymous usage analytics (if enabled) | Improve the site | Legitimate interest |
| Ad-related cookies | Serve relevant ads | Consent via cookie banner |

## Cookies

- **Google AdSense** may use cookies to personalize ads. You can opt out at [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- We do **not** use tracking pixels, fingerprinting or cross-site tracking beyond advertising partners' standard behavior.

## Your rights

You may request deletion of your newsletter subscription or contact history at any time: **hello@chunkscope.com**. Unsubscribe links are in every newsletter email.

## Children

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Minecraft content is informational; no accounts, profiles or user-generated storage exists on this site.

## Changes

Policy updates are posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

# DMCA & Copyright

ChunkScope respects intellectual property rights and expects users to do the same. All artwork on this site is original or AI-generated; game mechanics are factual information and not protected expression.

## Reporting copyrighted content

If you believe content on ChunkScope infringes your copyright, send a notice to **hello@chunkscope.com** including:

1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed.
2. The exact URL(s) of the allegedly infringing material.
3. Your contact information (name, email, address).
4. A statement of good-faith belief that the use is not authorized.
5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and you own the rights (or are authorized to act).
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

## Counter-notice

If you believe your content was removed in error, reply with the same details plus a statement of consent to jurisdiction. We reinstate content within 10 business days unless the original complainant files legal action.

## Process

Notices are reviewed within 48 hours. Valid notices result in removal of the specific material — not the entire site — and we keep records of all notices as required by law.

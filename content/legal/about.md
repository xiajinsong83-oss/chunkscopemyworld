# About ChunkScope

ChunkScope is a free collection of Minecraft tools and guides — seed maps, structure finders, ore locators, farm & redstone calculators, creative editors and version-locked tutorials. Everything runs in your browser, no signup, no downloads.

## What we do differently

- **Version-locked content**: every tool and guide states the exact Minecraft version it targets, so you never follow outdated mechanics.
- **Exclusive calculators**: farm output, spawn rates and redstone planning tools that don't exist on other sites.
- **Open coverage**: Java *and* Bedrock, 1.18 through the latest snapshot.

# How do I find a stronghold without eyes of ender?

**Strongholds** are the underground fortresses holding the End portal. They generate at **fixed positions determined by your seed**, so you can find them without throwing a single eye of ender.

## How strongholds generate

- **Java**: 3 strongholds spawn in a ring around world spawn (about 1,000+ blocks out), each at a seed-derived position. More exist further out in some versions.
- **Bedrock**: strongholds generate at set distances and angles from spawn.

## Fastest way: use a seed map

Enter your seed in our [Seed Map](/apps/seed-map/) or [Structure Finder](/apps/structure-finder/), filter to **Stronghold**, and get the exact coordinates of every one — including the End portal room search. No triangulation needed.

## Why this beats eyes of ender

Throwing eyes is slow, costs pearls, and eyes only point to the **nearest** stronghold. A seed map finds all of them instantly and never runs out.

## Digging tips

1. Strongholds are usually **Y 20–60**, often under forests or hills.
2. Dig a vertical shaft to the stronghold X/Z, then tunnel toward the portal room.
3. The **portal room** has a lava-filled frame — light all 12 with eyes of ender to activate it.

# How do I find slime chunks without mods?

Slime chunks are fixed per seed (10% of chunks), so you never need mods to find them.

## Method 1 — Seed Map (fastest)

Paste your seed into our [Slime Chunk Finder](/apps/slime-chunk-finder/) and get every slime chunk instantly, including Bedrock.

## Method 2 — Dig test

1. Pick a chunk you suspect (any chunk border you can see with F3+G on Java).
2. Dig a 3×3 shaft to **Y = 10–30** and light it fully.
3. Wait 10 minutes AFK. If slimes appear, you're in a slime chunk.

This only works below Y = 40 — above that, slimes never spawn in chunks.

## Method 3 — Swamp trick (Java only)

At night, slimes spawn on the surface in swamps (light level 7 or less) regardless of chunks. Clear a 20×20 swamp platform, light the edges, and any slime you see marks nearby slime territory. Slow, but zero tools needed.

## Bedrock note

Bedrock slime chunks use the **same seed math** since 1.18, so our finder works for both editions. Pre-1.18 Bedrock worlds are the only exception.

# How do I find an ancient city in Minecraft?

**Ancient cities** are huge underground structures added in 1.19. They only spawn inside **deep dark** biomes, deep underground, and hold the best loot — and the **Warden** guards them.

## Where ancient cities generate

- Only in **deep dark** biomes
- Almost always at **Y = -51** (the city floor) and below
- Fixed grid positions per seed (like other structures)

## How to find one for your seed

Use our [Structure Finder](/apps/structure-finder/) or [Seed Map](/apps/seed-map/). Enter your seed, filter to **Ancient City**, and you'll get every possible city location with coordinates — no blind digging required. Works on Java and Bedrock.

## How to explore safely

1. **Don't trigger sculk shriekers** — they summon the Warden.
2. Move on **wool** or place wool to block vibrations.
3. Bring a **Swift Sneak** enchantment (available in the city's own chests).
4. Grab the **Swift Sneak / Silence / Warden** loot and leave via a safe path.

## Loot worth hunting

Ancient cities contain **Swift Sneak and Silence enchanted books**, **Netherite upgrade** smithing templates, enchanted diamonds and echo shards — among the best loot in the game.

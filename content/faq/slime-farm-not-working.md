# Why is my slime farm not working?

Slime farms are finicky — a farm that "should" work often produces nothing for one of these reasons. Check them in order.

## 1. You're not in a slime chunk

Slimes only spawn in **10% of chunks** (the "slime chunks"). Building anywhere else produces nothing. Verify your location with our [Slime Chunk Finder](/apps/slime-chunk-finder/) — it marks every slime chunk for your seed on Java and Bedrock.

## 2. You're above Y = 40

Slimes spawn in slime chunks **only below Y = 40**. A platform at Y 30 works; one at Y 50 won't.

## 3. No valid spawn space

Slimes need:
- A **solid floor** (not slabs the slime can't spawn on)
- **3 blocks of air** above the floor (slimes are up to 3 blocks tall, but only spawn if the space allows the small ones)
- A **dark** area (or at least no hostile-mob fill)

## 4. Mob cap / spawn radius issues

You must be within **128 blocks** of the farm, and at least **~20 blocks** below or above the spawning platform to kill slimes without stopping spawns. Light up surrounding caves to free the mob cap.

## 5. AFK position wrong

Stand ~24+ blocks from the platform so slimes don't pile up, but within 128 so they spawn. See our [slime farm guide](/guides/farms/slime-farm-guide/) for a complete AFK setup.

# How do I find a village in Minecraft fast?

Villages aren't random — every seed has **fixed village positions** determined by the world generator. That means you can locate all of them without exploring.

## Where villages generate

Villages generate in biomes like **plains, desert, savanna, taiga and snowy plains**. Their spacing follows a grid (roughly 32–48 chunks apart), so for a given seed they appear at predictable points.

## Fastest way: use a structure finder

Paste your seed into our [Structure Finder](/apps/structure-finder/) (or the [Seed Map](/apps/seed-map/)) and you'll get every village location with coordinates — plains, desert, savanna and taiga variants. Works on **Java and Bedrock**.

## Quick tips

- Villages are easiest to spot from the air; look for the **iron golem spawn area** or the well.
- Trade with villagers for **Mending** and diamond gear.
- A village near spawn is one of the best **long-term bases** — see our [village base guide](/guides/survival/village-upgrade-guide/).

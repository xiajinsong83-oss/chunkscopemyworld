# What is a seed in Minecraft and how do I use one?

A **seed** is a number (or text) that the world generator uses to build your entire world. **Every seed creates a unique but repeatable world** — enter the same seed and you get the exact same terrain, biomes and structure positions.

## How seeds work

- The seed is converted to a number by the game.
- That number drives all the noise and structure algorithms.
- Two worlds with the same seed are **identical** in generation (Java and Bedrock 1.18+ align for most seeds).

## How to use a seed

1. **Create a world with a seed**: in world creation, click "More Options" (Java) or "Seed" (Bedrock) and type/paste it.
2. **Find your current seed**: press F3 on Java and look at "Seed"; on Bedrock use `/seed` in a world you can command.

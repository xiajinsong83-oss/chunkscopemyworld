# My villager breeder isn't working — what's wrong?

A villager breeder fails for a handful of common reasons. Work through this checklist from the most likely cause first.

## 1. Not enough beds

Villagers need **one spare bed per baby**. If the breeder cell has fewer beds than villagers, breeding stops. Add more beds with 2+ blocks of air above them.

## 2. Missing food

Breeding requires both parents to be **"willing"** — they need to have picked up food recently (bread, carrots, potatoes, beetroot). Throw food to both villagers and wait.

## 3. Village population limit

A breeder near an existing village shares the **village cap** (about 35 villagers per village, 10 per door-based area). Move the breeder at least **50–70 blocks** from any village.

## 4. No valid "door" / job sites

Villagers need a **workstation or door** to register the village. Place a job block (e.g. composter) for each parent.

## 5. Mob griefing off

If `mobGriefing` is off, villagers **can't pick up food** and won't breed. Turn it back on or use a design that bypasses it.

# What Y level is best for netherite (ancient debris) in 1.21?

**Ancient debris** — the only source of netherite — is most common at **Y = 15** in the Nether, with the best practical mining range from **Y = 8 to Y = 22**.

## Best levels for ancient debris

| Layer | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Y = 15** | Peak | Most debris per chunk |
| Y 8–22 | Best range | Consistent yields |
| Y 14–16 | Sweet spot | Common recommendation |
| Below Y 8 / above Y 22 | Lower | Fewer veins |

## How to mine netherite efficiently

1. **Tunnel at Y = 15** in a straight line (branch mine every 2–3 blocks).
2. Use **bed mining** or **TNT** to clear large areas fast — beds are cheap and explode reliably in the Nether.
3. Ancient debris is **blast-resistant**, so explosions safely expose it without destroying it.
4. Smelt debris in a furnace to get netherite scrap (4 scrap + 4 gold → 1 netherite ingot).

## Tips

- Debris spawns **regardless of your seed** — density is uniform, so no seed hunting needed.
- Bring **Fire Resistance** potions; lava is everywhere at these depths.
- Netherite upgrades weapons/armor and gives knockback resistance.

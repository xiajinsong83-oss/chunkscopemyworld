# What Y level is best for diamonds in Minecraft 1.21?

In Minecraft 1.18–1.21, **diamonds are most common at Y = -59** (the lowest block above bedrock). This is true on both Java and Bedrock.

## Best mining levels for diamonds

| Ore | Best Y level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Diamond** | **Y = -59** | Peaks at -59, solid rates from about -58 to -50 |
| Deepslate diamond | same range | Deeper layers are deepslate |
| Best strategy | -59 branch mine | Strip-mine long tunnels at -59 |

Diamond ore generates between Y 16 and -64, but the frequency curve rises sharply as you go deeper, maxing out at **Y = -59**.

## Mining notes

- Bring **Fortune III** pickaxes to multiply drops.
- At these depths light every tunnel — lava lakes are common around Y -54 to -59.
- Use **blast resistance** or careful tunneling around ancient cities nearby.

# What Y level is best for iron in Minecraft 1.21?

In Minecraft 1.18–1.21, **iron has two best Y levels**: **Y = 15** (main peak, most practical) and **Y = 232** (second peak, high in the sky).

## Best mining levels for iron

| Peak | Best Y level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Main peak** | **Y = 15** | Most iron ore, easy to reach |
| Secondary peak | Y = 232 | Above mountains, worth it if you're mining near the top |
| Between | Y 20–90 | Low rates |

## Why Y = 15

Iron ore frequency peaks sharply at Y 15 and stays strong across Y 13–19. This is the standard spot for strip-mining iron, and it's easy to reach by caving down from bedrock level caves.

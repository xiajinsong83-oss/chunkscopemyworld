# My iron farm stopped producing — what's wrong?

Check these in order — one of them is almost always the cause.

## 1. Villagers aren't sleeping

Golems only spawn after villagers sleep and gossip. Make sure every villager has a **claimed bed** with 2 blocks of air above it. A villager that can't sleep stops the whole cycle.

**Fix:** break and re-place beds, or add a third cell. Verify at night — all villagers must lie down.

## 2. Cats are eating the mob cap

Each village caps at ~10 cats before any golems. If you see more than 4–5 cats, they're stealing spawns.

**Fix:** kill the extras, or raise your golem platform so cats can't path up.

## 3. Spawn platform is too high

Golems spawn on the highest solid surface in range. If your roof is 5+ blocks above the platform, golems spawn on the roof instead and never fall into the kill chamber.

**Fix:** keep the roof within 3 blocks of the platform, or light up the roof.

## 4. Simulation distance too low

On Bedrock, farms need simulation distance **6+**; Java needs the chunks loaded. If you AFK too far away, the farm freezes.

**Fix:** AFK within 32 blocks, and check your server's simulation distance setting.

## 5. Workstations broke

Villagers must claim a workstation to count as workers. A broken barrel or lectern resets them to unemployed — and the village population math drops.

**Fix:** place 4–6 spare workstations around the cells and never trade with farm villagers.

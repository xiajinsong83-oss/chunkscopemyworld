# How do I find my spawn chunks in Minecraft?

**Spawn chunks** are the chunks around your world spawn that **stay loaded** even when no player is near them. In single-player this lets farms, redstone and mob farms keep running while you explore.

## Where are the spawn chunks?

World spawn is determined by the **seed** — it's the place you first appear. The loaded spawn area is roughly a **19×19 chunk square** (about 304×304 blocks) centred on the spawn chunk.

## How to find them for your seed

1. **From your current world**: press **F3** and note the coordinates where you originally spawned, or find the world spawn on the map.
2. **From a seed**: paste your seed into our [Spawn Chunk Finder](/apps/world-finders/spawn-chunk-finder/) to see the exact spawn point and the loaded chunk square instantly — Java and Bedrock.

## Why spawn chunks matter

- **Mob farms** and **iron farms** built in spawn chunks keep producing while you're elsewhere.
- **Redstone** clocks and sorting systems keep running.
- This is why players often move farms to spawn before exploring far away.

## Note for Bedrock

Bedrock's spawn chunks stay loaded differently and are smaller in practice, so always verify with our [Spawn Chunk Finder](/apps/world-finders/spawn-chunk-finder/) before relying on them.
